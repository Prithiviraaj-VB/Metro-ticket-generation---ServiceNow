🚇 Metro Ticket Booking System using ServiceNow

A digital metro ticketing solution built on the **ServiceNow** platform that enables fast, accessible, and automated ticket generation through **QR codes**, replacing queues and paper tickets with a seamless cashless experience.

 📌 Overview

Traditional metro ticketing involves long queues, manual cash handling, and paper waste. This project digitizes the entire booking journey, from selecting stations to paying and receiving a scannable QR ticket, while giving metro operators automated workflows and data insights.

 🎯 Objectives

 1. Enhance Commuter Convenience
Provide user-friendly digital ticketing through a mobile app, QR codes, and WhatsApp-based booking to reduce wait times and simplify the process.

 2. Increase Operational Efficiency
Adopt Automated Fare Collection (AFC) to minimize manual intervention, reduce errors, and lower operational costs.

 3. Promote Digital Payment Adoption
Encourage cashless transactions through UPI, credit/debit cards, and mobile wallets to improve revenue collection.

 4. Facilitate Data-Driven Decision Making
Use analytics from digital transactions to understand passenger behavior, peak usage times, and route optimization opportunities.

 5. Support Environmental Sustainability
Reduce paper usage by moving to digital tickets, aligning with eco-friendly and sustainability goals.

 ✨ Key Features

 🎫 **QR Code Ticket Generation**: unique, scannable ticket generated after successful payment
 📱 **Multi-Channel Booking**: mobile app, web portal, and WhatsApp
 💳 **Digital Payments**: UPI, credit/debit cards, and wallets
 🤖 **Automated Workflows**: booking, validation, and notifications handled by ServiceNow Flow Designer
 🔁 **AFC Integration**: QR validation at entry/exit gates
 📊 **Dashboards & Reports**: ridership trends, peak hours, revenue, and route insights
 🔔 **Notifications**: email/SMS/WhatsApp confirmations and ticket delivery
 🌱 **Paperless Travel**: zero paper tickets
